About Farmer Guide AI

An intelligent farming assistant designed to help farmers make better decisions using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

What is Farmer Guide AI?

Farmer Guide AI is an AI-powered smart farming assistant developed to provide useful agricultural information and intelligent predictions through a simple web-based platform.

The system combines Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and web technologies to assist farmers in understanding crop-related problems and making data-driven farming decisions.

Our goal is to make modern agricultural technology easier to access and easier to understand for farmers.

What Farmer Guide AI Provides

The platform is designed around practical agricultural assistance.

🌱

Crop Recommendation

Recommends suitable crops using agricultural parameters and a trained Machine Learning model.

🐞

AI Disease Detection

Allows users to upload a crop leaf image and uses an AI model to predict possible plant diseases.

☀️

Weather Forecast

Provides weather information to help farmers plan irrigation, sowing, harvesting and other farming activities.

💧

Fertilizer Recommendation

Suggests suitable fertilizers based on crop requirements and agricultural parameters for healthy plant growth.

₹

Market Prices

Provides crop and market price information to help farmers understand market trends and make better selling decisions.

🤖

AI Farming Assistant

Provides an interactive AI-based assistant for agriculture-related questions, guidance and information.

Our Objective

The main objective of Farmer Guide AI is to combine agricultural knowledge with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to create a useful and user-friendly smart farming platform.

The project aims to support farmers by providing intelligent assistance through technology while keeping the interface simple and accessible.
